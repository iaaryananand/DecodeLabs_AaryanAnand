# Week 4 Project — E-commerce Sales Visualization

## Overview

This project presents e-commerce sales data through Excel PivotTables, charts, and a dashboard. The visualizations explore sales by product and referral source, monthly sales trends, and order status.

## Objective

The objective was to turn the analysis into clear visuals, highlight useful patterns, and explain what the findings could mean for the business.

## Tools and Methods

- Microsoft Excel
- PivotTables
- Bar charts and a line chart
- Data visualization and storytelling

## Analysis Performed

- Visualized total sales by product.
- Compared total sales across referral sources.
- Charted monthly sales from 2023 through June 2025.
- Compared order counts by status.
- Arranged the charts and findings in a dashboard.

## Key Findings

- The dataset contains 1,200 orders.
- Total sales were ₹1,264,761.96, and Chair had the highest total sales among the products.
- Instagram had the highest total sales among the referral sources.
- Monthly sales were highest in June 2024 and lowest in April 2023.
- The 2025 data covers January through June only, so it represents a partial year.
